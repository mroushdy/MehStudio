# Shared rear chamber and resistive slots: capability boundary

Build 14 now includes an independent [compact-source passive-slot fixture](cardioid-study.md). This document remains the contract for the more complete horn/cabinet field model; the compact fixture does not qualify that full model.

The editor's shared chamber is a common rear compliance for identical coherently driven cone mids. Sealed and bass-reflex loading are supported. A rectangular bass-reflex duct is not a passive resistive slot, and the rear vent loss-Q assumption is not a porous-material model. Neither reduced network predicts combined front/rear far-field radiation or passive cardioid behavior. The rear enclosure controls now state this distinction beside the loading choice.

Fulcrum's [manufacturer explanation](https://www.fulcrum-acoustic.com/education/passive-cardioid-technology) describes coordinated driver placement, enclosure dimensions, ports and calibrated resistive elements. Its [published technical disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10123111B2/en) also describes coupled front and rear acoustic paths. These sources motivate the coupled-system requirements below; they do not validate a MEH Studio geometry or supply its material impedance data. Sources checked 30 September 2026.

## What can be handed off today

The acoustic export kit can build one connected exterior air boundary containing the horn, cone fronts, enclosure and actual rear ducts. With **Rear-vent velocity bases** enabled, each physical vent inlet has a distinct source tag (151–156); each cone retains its own source (101–106). The saved voltage, axes, projected areas, source mapping and observation frame are retained. The ABEC and Boundary Lab projects expose prescribed velocity bases, with rear vents muted by default. They do not solve a shared rear cavity, a resistive sheet, driver motors or slot flow under the saved electrical drive. Native project loading and mesh checks cannot establish a radiation result.

The physical rear duct is already included in the mesh. Adding its complete lumped inertance or a second end correction to the same meshed passage would double-count air loading. See [the current geometry/domain contract](acoustic-mesh-formulation.md), [Boundary Lab source mapping](boundary-lab-export.md) and [runner instructions](../mesh-export/README.md).

## Required extension before a cardioid prediction

| Requirement | Concrete contract and verification |
| --- | --- |
| Physical slot and treatment | Store each opening's position, outline, thickness, connected rear domain, material/treatment area and provenance. Distinguish a distributed treatment over an aperture from a duct with a terminal sheet. Preserve multiple independent slots and their exterior positions. No equivalent round slot may silently replace them. |
| Complex acoustic impedance | Declare a frequency-dependent pressure-jump/normal-velocity relation in Pa·s/m, or a pressure-jump/volume-flow relation in Pa·s/m³, with conversion by effective area. State phasor convention, direction, RMS/peak convention, temperature, flow/level range, sample construction and measurement/model provenance. Check passive dissipation under those conventions; a constant arbitrary resistance is insufficient evidence of real material behavior. |
| Connected shared rear air | Couple the rear sides of every cone and all slot inner faces to one common pressure only where a lumped compliance is justified. Otherwise solve the rear cavity as a field domain. Include actual net air volume, diaphragm displacement directions and pressure difference across each cone; do not attach one independent full shared volume to every cone. |
| Common exterior | Obtain complex self/mutual loading and complex pressure transfers for every front-cone and slot source on the same exterior geometry and coordinate frame. Slot/horn spacing, diffraction and propagation phase must survive. Two independently normalized magnitude plots cannot be summed into a directivity prediction. |
| Coupled electrical drive | Solve all cone motors, rear air, slot impedance and exterior loads together. Preserve exact driver variants, source phase/polarity and voltage. A slot's prescribed unit-velocity transfer is a basis for this calculation, not its solved velocity. |
| Domain bookkeeping | Version the manifest with connected-domain IDs, paired interface IDs/normals, impedance surfaces and source roles. Explicitly record where duct propagation, end corrections and losses live. Current exterior-only inlet caps must become declared coupling interfaces when rear air is added; no capped/disconnected air or duplicated passage terms. Reject missing/stale matrices and unsupported topology. |
| Qualification | Check topology, normalization, reciprocal coupling where applicable, passivity and energy balance, and frequency/mesh/basis convergence on a tractable reference. Then compare calibrated complex impedance and polar response against a prototype, including the rear direction and several levels. Source/native parsing alone is not acoustic qualification. |

No resistance control or cardioid score is added in this release. The useful bounded feature is the existing independently mapped rear-vent radiation basis, now easier to find and explicitly distinguished from a coupled prediction. The separate product-architecture study can propose hypotheses; this contract defines what would make them calculable and testable.
